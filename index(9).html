Useful information to collect before creating a new product page:

  • Product or project name
  • Page title
  • Product category
  • Target age group
  • Short product description
  • Number of instruction videos
  • Title of every video
  • Video file name or URL
  • Preferred video order
  • Safety instructions or warnings
  • Product image or cover image
  • Downloadable manual or PDF link
  • Troubleshooting information
