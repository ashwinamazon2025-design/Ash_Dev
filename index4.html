OTP Login

Access is restricted to the single authorized email below.

Authorized email ashwin.geralds@outlook.com
OTP login needs a one-time EmailJS setup. Add your EmailJS Service ID, Template ID and Public Key in the CONFIG section near the bottom of this file. EmailJS is used only to send the login code; your products, JSON files and videos remain in GitHub.
In your EmailJS template, set the To Email field permanently to ashwin.geralds@outlook.com. Do not make the recipient a user-editable template variable. This is a lightweight browser-side OTP gate; keep your GitHub token repository-scoped and session-only.
